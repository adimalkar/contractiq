    Termnova — Enterprise AI Contract Intelligence, Knowledge Graph & Clause Diffing

    Termnova is an advanced, production-grade legal intelligence platform. Built for corporate legal teams, law firms, procurement managers, and contract administrators to analyze agreements, uncover risks, detect clause modifications, and map multi-contract relationships.

    Core Capabilities & Features

    • Contract Studio & Hybrid RAG: Natural language query answering with reciprocal rank fusion (BM25 + Dense Vectors) and verbatim page-level source citations.
    • Document Map & Contract Knowledge Graph: AI-powered entity extraction mapping parent Master Services Agreements (MSAs), Statements of Work (SOWs), Amendments, counterparty entities, and governing jurisdictions with D3.js force layouts.
    • Clause Redline & Side-by-Side Diff: Semantic difference highlighting detecting modifications in financial terms, liability caps, indemnification clauses, and termination windows.
    • Document Vault & Vector Repository: Secure multi-format ingestion supporting PDF, DOCX, TXT, and Markdown files with pgvector partition isolation.
    • Enterprise Guardrails & Safety: Real-time PII redaction, API secret scrubbing, and zero AI model training retention guarantee.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    What is Termnova?

    Termnova is an open enterprise AI contract intelligence platform engineered with LangGraph multi-step reasoning agents, hybrid RAG, and PostgreSQL graph topology.

    How does Termnova protect confidential legal documents?

    All contract data is processed with zero-retention policies. No customer contract data is ever used to train third-party foundation models. Real-time guardrails sanitize sensitive PII and API keys.

    What contract types are supported?

    Termnova analyzes MSAs, SOWs, NDAs, Amendments, Addenda, Commercial Leases, Vendor Agreements, and Employment Contracts.
