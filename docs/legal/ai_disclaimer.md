# Termnova — Responsible AI & Legal Disclaimer

**Important Notice for All Users and Organizations**

### 1. Artificial Intelligence Limitations
Termnova utilizes Large Language Models (LLMs) and advanced Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) to parse, index, search, and synthesize legal contracts. While Termnova is built with multi-factor grounding, strict citation requirements, heuristic entailment checkers, and confidence scoring:
* **Probabilistic Output**: AI systems are statistical language models and may occasionally produce incomplete, ambiguous, or imprecise interpretations of complex contractual provisions.
* **Jurisdictional Nuances**: Contractual enforceability varies substantially across states, countries, and regulatory frameworks. Termnova's algorithms do not account for local jurisdictional case law or statutory updates unless explicitly provided in the uploaded document corpus.

---

### 2. Not Legal Advice & No Attorney-Client Privilege
* **Software Tool Only**: Termnova is a software tool, NOT a licensed attorney, law practice, or legal services provider.
* **No Privilege**: Use of Termnova does not create an attorney-client relationship, fiduciary duty, or legal privilege of any kind.
* **Mandatory Legal Review**: You should never execute a contract, terminate an agreement, waive a legal claim, or initiate litigation based solely on outputs generated by this platform. Always consult qualified, licensed legal counsel.

---

### 3. Built-In Guardrails & Safety Architecture
Termnova implements layered guardrails to maximize output accuracy and security:
* **Entailment Verifier**: Every sentence generated is audited against original source text for factual overlap.
* **PII & Secret Redaction**: Automatic redaction of SSNs, phone numbers, API keys, and connection credentials.
* **Confidence Scoring**: Answers are assigned a confidence score based on retrieval depth, ranking relevance, and entailment overlap.
* **Strict Citation Mandate**: Claims must reference specific `[Source N]` chunk anchors from the original document.
